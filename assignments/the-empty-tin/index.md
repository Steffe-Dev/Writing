Piet stared into his cup of coffee. It was a muddy brown colour. Hester used the amount usually reserved for one cup to make the pot for both of them, to save money. He looked up, and found her staring straight at him.

"The rent tin is empty," she said.

"So it is."

"Well? Is there anything you want to tell me?"

Piet sighed. Hester resembled a kettle, slowly bubbling towards a boiling point. She pointed at his coat, which hung over an empty chair.

"Do you want to explain to me why this tin is empty, and that pocket over there is full?"

"I'm visiting Charlie this weekend for his birthday. I do it every year."

"How nice! You'll be eating cake and biscuits while I get kicked out into the snow."

Piet's jaw tightened. He walked over to his coat, took out the ticket, and tossed it into the tin in one fluid motion.
