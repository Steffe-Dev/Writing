# The assignment

en · Probe 1 of 4: dramatization

## Situation

Ivo is at his stall, Brine, when hail starts on the tin. A stranger's crate of eels is still on the open board, and the ice in it is going soft. The stranger stands with their back turned, counting coins into a palm. Ivo has to lift the crate off that board and set it on the dry boards under the canvas. This is only the first movement. Stop when the crate is under the canvas, or when the ice goes. Do not write the talk about the money.

## Length and form

150–400 words. Fragment.

## Constraints

Write above the include line in index.md. One scene. No notes to yourself.

## Checks

1. The first sentence contains a physical action.
2. By the end, the crate's place or the person's grip on it has changed.
3. The change follows from an earlier action in the scene, not from a new wish announced at the end.
4. No sentence tells the reader what the person feels without a visible action or object attached.
5. The prose above the include line is between 150 and 400 words.
