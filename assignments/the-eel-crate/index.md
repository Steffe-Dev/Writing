I clutched my hands around the crate, attempting to keep the lid shut with my thumbs. Do eels, I wonder, experience death the same way we do? Looking at their stupefied expressions, as they bathed in the melting ice inside, I could not help doubting whether they do. Granted, they desperately wanted to escape from their cramped, wooden prison, but I blame their captor for that. They seemed to be unbothered by their use in constructing a crude drum being played by a hailstorm. I pulled with all the strength I could muster. As the crate travelled upwards, it became significantly lighter about halfway towards my chest. I looked down at the same stupefied expressions as before, scattered across the ground. Then I looked at their captor, staring me down over his shoulder with an expression altogether different from stupefied. Was I about to experience death in she same way the eels did?
