Can I say that a person, in their hubris, thought that they would never stumble. Then, one day, they proceeded to fall, but stood up again in an act of character development. To this day, they strive to mind their step more carefully.

That covers all the bases of a narrative curve, right? My only concern is that it is too long for contemporary readers.
